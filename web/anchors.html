  1. The video cuts every break: between half-innings and at mid-inning pitching changes. Each row is the first pitch after a break. Play the video, and when the pitcher throws the highlighted one, press Mark first pitch (or the M key). It moves on to the next.
  2. Jump near the next one skips to roughly where the next one is, so you don't have to watch the whole game.
  3. Rows marked estimated were worked out from the marks around them. Press Go on one, watch for the pitch, and re-mark it if it is off.
  4. Missed it by a bit? Use −1s / +1s on that row, or Go to replay from the marked time and check.
  5. When you have as many as you want (all of them is best, a few still helps), press Download anchors.csv and save it over config/anchors.csv in the project.
